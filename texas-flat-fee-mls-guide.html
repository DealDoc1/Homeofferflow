What this request does—and does not—do

A flat-fee MLS interest request gives HomeOfferFlow the basics needed to route a seller to the appropriate next conversation. It may help organize property facts and timing, but it does not create a listing, select a provider, establish a brokerage relationship, or order a paid service.

Any licensed brokerage, title, legal, or other regulated work remains with the responsible professional. You stay in control of whether to proceed after scope and availability are confirmed.

Before you request options

Clear answers for Texas sellers.

Does this create an MLS listing?

No. This is a no-obligation interest request only. It does not create a listing agreement, listing, brokerage relationship, or provider selection.

What happens after I submit?

You receive a seller plan and your requested support path is available for a separate scope-and-availability conversation. Final pricing and any responsible provider are confirmed before paid work begins.

Do I have to know every property detail now?

No. Start with the basics you know. Keep questions visible for follow-up rather than guessing or treating this guide as professional advice.
