Electronic Communications and E-Sign Consent

Version 2.0 · Last updated: July 29, 2026

By selecting the related acceptance box or using an electronic-signature workflow, you agree to receive and use records electronically as described below.

1. Electronic Records and Notices

You consent to receive electronically these terms, disclosures, receipts, invoices, account notices, subscription notices, document links, signature requests, support communications, and other information relating to HomeOfferFlow. Electronic communications may be delivered through the website, account dashboard, email, or a third-party electronic-signature provider.

2. Electronic Signatures

When you request, send, access, or sign a document electronically, you agree to conduct that transaction electronically to the extent permitted by law. You authorize HomeOfferFlow to transmit documents and signer information to the electronic-signature provider selected for the workflow. You remain responsible for confirming that electronic signatures are appropriate, authorized, complete, and legally sufficient for your transaction.

3. Hardware and Software Requirements

To access and retain electronic records, you need a current internet-connected device, a current browser, a valid email address, and the ability to download, view, print, or save PDF documents. You should keep your email address current and check spam, junk, and filtered folders. You may print or save a copy of these records for your files.

4. Paper Copies and Updating Contact Information

You may request a paper copy of a HomeOfferFlow record that we can reasonably provide by emailing support@homeofferflow.com. We may charge a reasonable fee where permitted by law. You are responsible for updating your contact information through your account or by contacting support.

5. Withdrawal of Consent

You may withdraw consent to general electronic communications by discontinuing use of the Services and contacting support. Withdrawal does not affect communications, records, transactions, or signatures already completed, and may prevent us from providing features that require electronic delivery. Certain transactional or legal notices may still be delivered as permitted by law.

6. Your Responsibilities

HomeOfferFlow does not verify every signer’s identity, authority, capacity, intent, or receipt of a signature request. You must review all document and recipient details before sending, review the completed signed document and available audit materials, and retain appropriate copies. Do not rely on HomeOfferFlow as your only recordkeeping system.
