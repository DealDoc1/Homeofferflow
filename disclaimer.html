HomeOfferFlow is not acting as your real estate broker, sales agent, fiduciary, transaction coordinator, title representative, lender, or negotiating representative. Use does not create a buyer-representation agreement, listing agreement, agency, intermediary, fiduciary, broker-client, or other representation relationship. HomeOfferFlow uses information and selections provided by users to populate, assemble, save, or route documents. You must review the complete rendered document—not only an interview summary—before it is sent, signed, submitted, or relied upon.
