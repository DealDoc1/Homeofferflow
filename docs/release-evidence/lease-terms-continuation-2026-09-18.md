# Temporary-lease special-provision completeness - September 18, 2026

## Defect and local correction

Both TREC 16-7 Buyer and TREC 15-7 Seller Temporary Residential Lease overlays
used `fitted_blank_entries` for Paragraph 11. On overflow, that helper replaces
the end of the entered terms with an ellipsis. A single very long unbroken
token could also yield no entries. The full-text review did not prevent these
losses in the generated signing packet.

With the same 60-term synthetic input, the old fitting helper omits the final
term and ends in `...` for both forms. The new Paragraph 11 helper retains
short terms in the existing eight-point blanks, or places a continuation
reference on the lease and reproduces the whole text on labeled pages. It
does not summarize the text, draft new terms, or alter the source PDF.

Continuation headings identify Buyer or Seller Temporary Residential Lease,
Paragraph 11, and the property. Appended pages follow the repair and non-realty
continuations; existing source-form signatures and uploaded-document offsets
are preserved. Required buyer initials are added to new pages, with seller
initials only for sellers already included by the existing execution plan.
No new recipient is inferred or invited.
