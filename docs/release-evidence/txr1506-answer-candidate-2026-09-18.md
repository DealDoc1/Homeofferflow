## Findings and corrections

The six-page 06-15-26 source was read and measured. The old renderer placed
consumer names inside the final SignWell signature regions. Character-count
wrapping allowed a 1,000-character Other notice to exceed its two available
lines and enter the printed explanatory/acknowledgment area. Broker and second
consumer names also touched their source underlines. The consumer-identification
headers on pages 2-6 were empty.

The renderer now uses `lib/txr1506_answers.py` for measured-width placement:

- Consumer headers: x=234, y=756, width=268 PDF points, within the source's
  x=231.89..504.034 identification blank.
- Other notice: two blanks x=38, width=534, baselines y=324/311, above the
  printed underscore rules and below the Other heading.
- Broker printed name: x=38, y=214, width=248, above the source rule whose
  top-origin position is 581.83.
- Unsigned consumer names: x=38, width=248, baselines y=109/75; those values
  are omitted from signature areas in the actual signing copy, using the
  existing `_for_signing` server path. Names remain in the page headers.

Values fit at 8-to-7 points or continue in full on an attached answer
continuation. Unicode uses the existing embedded-font fallback. Long consumer
names appear once each in the exhibit rather than as both a combined list and
duplicate individual entries. Attachment language says Consumer and consumer
notice; no representation or compensation terms are added or inferred.

The actual rendered page count drives added required initials for the selected
consumer(s) and broker/associate. All existing base-page provider positions are
unchanged. Simultaneous signing is preserved. Render and signing-map revisions
were advanced to prevent treating an older prepared request as this new copy.
