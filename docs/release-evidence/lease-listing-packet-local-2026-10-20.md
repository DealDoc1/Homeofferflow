## Outcome and scope

The lease listing now uses the shared PDF composer and send-review contract,
with lease-specific labels, not a relabeled sale packet. It can append the
acting account's verified saved IABS exactly once. Missing related documents
remain visible as pending, without preventing the prepared documents from
being sent. The API binds review to the actual contents before sending and
retains simultaneous invitations and the existing delivery retry journal.

The rental flood disclosure is always requested. The lease lead disclosure is
requested for pre-1978 or unconfirmed-age homes. Neither behavior fabricates a
landlord's disclosure answers or claims that an unprepared document is attached.
Lease composition rejects the sale Seller's Disclosure binding. Official Texas
REALTORS FAQs identify TXR-2015 for rental flood disclosure and TXR-2008 for the
lease lead disclosure; their real source adapters remain unfinished.

The shared IABS loader retains exact user/bucket/path/MIME checks. A genuine
404 is pending; wrong ownership or a temporary storage failure remains a
visible error, not a fabricated missing-file result. No alias gains access to
another account's private saved document.
