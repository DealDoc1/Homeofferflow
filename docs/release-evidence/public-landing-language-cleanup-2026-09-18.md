# Public landing-language cleanup — September 18, 2026

## Outcome

The homepage plus the buyer, agent/broker, investor and partner landing pages
now describe what the customer does instead of calling the experience a
“workflow.” The revised copy uses direct actions such as answering guided
questions, reviewing what is included, preparing supported documents and
appearing at relevant decision points.

This removes internal product language from the visible acquisition pages
without changing the transaction choices, URLs, analytics event names,
structured data, prices, legal boundaries, authentication, checkout, document
generation or signature delivery.

## Verification

Eighteen focused public-entry, landing-layout and low-noise copy tests pass.
The low-noise check parses rendered page text while excluding scripts and
styles, then verifies that the homepage acquisition area and six primary
public landing pages—buyers, sellers, agents, investors, partners and
directory—do not show “workflow” to visitors.

This is **locally implemented and tested, not deployed or production visually
verified**. It adds no service, dependency, database work, API call or recurring
cost.
