# Purchase amount precision and financing text placement

## Status

Local implementation, runtime-tested, browser-to-PDF checked, and unsigned
PDF placement visually inspected. **Not pushed or deployed.** This is not
completed-signature QA or verification of the full production checkout flow.

## Corrected behavior

The cash-switch check from the loan-assumption interview reproduced a price
of 500000.55 becoming 500001. The collector rounded entered amounts, the
calculation helpers rounded whole dollars, and PDF formatting discarded cents.

- Preserve entered cents through collection for cash, conventional, FHA, VA,
  and USDA purchase offers. Round calculated defaults to cents, not dollars.
- Derive the contract cash portion with decimal arithmetic. For example,
  500000.55 minus 450000.44 is exactly 50000.11.
- Preserve cents in earnest money, option fees, concessions, brokerage amounts,
  home-warranty amounts, and the monetary fields using the shared formatter,
  including personal property, HOA, sale-contingency and backup addenda.
- Keep whole-dollar PDF values in their existing whole-dollar display style.
- Reject fractional-cent amounts rather than silently changing them. The
  interview validates its key monetary answers, including a populated down
  payment. Checkout validates populated monetary terms before initializing
  Stripe or saving a payable session. Product prices remain server-owned.
- Keep interest-rate precision separate from currency precision.
- Return a specific 400 response for a currency input error in PDF generation.

## Visual defect found during verification

Three-decimal percentage examples exposed old text coordinates that could
overlap printed percent signs, particularly conventional and FHA origination
charges and VA origination charges. The eight interest/origination entries now
use eight-point text inside the measured TREC 40-11 blanks. Independent bounds
tests cover both 6.125/1.125 and 12.875/10.125, including complete rendered
glyph bounds above the source rule. The source's invisible space glyphs are
excluded from text extraction, not edited in the source PDF.

No signature coordinates, original form content, provider configuration,
customer document or signed agreement was changed by this correction.

## Browser-to-PDF evidence

Used one temporary Chrome tab against a loopback-only bridge containing the
actual purchase-step markup, helpers and collector. The bridge uses synthetic
parties and the real PDF builder, not production services. It does not expose
the full application, authenticated storage, payments, maps, or signature sends.
The prescribed agent-browser CLI was unavailable; the connected browser was
used as the fallback. The browser verification skills required checking the
response and resulting artifact, not just whether the page loaded.

1. Conventional: entering 500000.55 price and 450000.44 loan showed 50000.11
   down payment. Submission and response retained those exact values, 5000.45
   earnest money, 250.99 option fee, 6.125 interest cap, and 1.125 origination
   cap. The generated 14-page PDF contains the exact currency strings.
2. Switch the same interview to cash: submission and response retained the
   500000.55 price/cash portion, cleared loan to zero, and produced a 12-page
   PDF with the exact price, earnest money and option fee.
3. Both calls returned `providerContacted:false` and a visible ready message.
   No customer email, charge, signature invitation or production write occurred.

The console recorded "Could not establish connection. Receiving end does not
exist." It did not prevent either successful request; its source was not
definitively identified, so no error-free-browser claim is made. Temporary
browser tab was closed and local server stopped. Private QA artifacts remain
untracked under `tmp/pdfs/currency-precision/` and `tmp/pdfs/currency-browser/`.
