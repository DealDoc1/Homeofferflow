# SEO and dynamic copy consistency — September 18, 2026

## Outcome

The homepage search schema, buyer-path FAQ content and founding-partner search
metadata now use the same concise customer language as the visible landing
pages. Search engines and customers who switch paths no longer receive stale
internal “workflow” wording after the public-page language cleanup.

The buyer copy now describes answering guided questions, reviewing the offer
summary and receiving a secure signature request. Partner metadata describes
placements at relevant customer decision points. Prices, checkout rules,
analytics, legal boundaries and application behavior are unchanged.

## Verification

Fifty-four focused discovery, dynamic-path, low-noise and audience-isolation
tests pass. They verify both removal of the stale phrases and presence of the
replacement customer actions. All five homepage and four partner JSON-LD
blocks also parse successfully after the copy changes.

This change is **locally implemented and tested**. It is not deployed or
production verified and adds no dependency, external service, database work,
browser action or recurring cost.
