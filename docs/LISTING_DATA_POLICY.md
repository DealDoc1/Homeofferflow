# HomeOfferFlow-managed listing-data policy

HomeOfferFlow does not ask an agent or brokerage to connect an MLS account,
share credentials, or complete a data-access activation in order to use the
product. The current product does not use MLS data.

Every user begins with an address. Address autocomplete and the guided
interview collect the details needed to prepare a packet. Listing facts are
never represented as MLS-verified unless HomeOfferFlow has centrally licensed
and operates that data source.

If a future product needs licensed listing data, HomeOfferFlow—not an agent or
brokerage customer—will obtain, fund, and operate the appropriate provider
relationship. The customer experience remains address-first and requires no
credential, authorization, or setup step.

Until centrally licensed data is worth the cost and can be operated within its
provider terms, offer review uses the terms entered in the interview and
plainly asks the user to confirm live listing facts before acting.
