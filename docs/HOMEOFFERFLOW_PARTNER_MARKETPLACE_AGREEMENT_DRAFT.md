# HomeOfferFlow Partner Marketplace Agreement

**Texas commercial draft — requires final Texas counsel review before use.**

This agreement is between HomeOfferFlow, `[legal entity and address]`, and the
Partner named in the Order Form. The Order Form/Stripe checkout controls the
selected category, market, tier, price, term, billing interval, and any express
exclusivity. The partner signs this agreement through SignWell after paid
onboarding; SignWell sends the final completed PDF to the signer and
`support@homeofferflow.com`.

1. **Placement.** HomeOfferFlow may display approved Partner information in its
   marketplace. Payment and onboarding do not guarantee publication. The
   placement is advertising inventory, not a promise of leads, transactions,
   revenue, ranking, referrals, endorsement, availability, or exclusivity.

2. **Editorial control and disclosure.** HomeOfferFlow may format, label,
   defer, reject, suspend, remove, or reposition a placement to protect users,
   comply with law, maintain a neutral directory, or enforce this agreement.
   It may label the placement “Sponsored,” “Paid Partner,” “Advertisement,” or
   similar. Partner will not imply HomeOfferFlow selected, endorsed, guaranteed,
   ranked, or recommends Partner. Users remain free to choose any provider.

3. **Partner promises.** Partner warrants its submitted information, licenses,
   qualifications, content, testimonials, offers, prices, trademarks, websites,
   and claims are current, accurate, substantiated, lawful, and not misleading.
   Partner alone is responsible for its services, personnel, licensing,
   insurance, advertising, communications, privacy, taxes, and professional
   compliance. Partner will promptly report material changes.

4. **No professional services by HomeOfferFlow.** HomeOfferFlow is not
   providing brokerage, lending, title, appraisal, insurance, inspection,
   construction, legal, tax, or other regulated professional services through
   this agreement. Nothing creates a referral, agency, fiduciary, brokerage, or
   preferred-provider relationship with users.

5. **Content license.** Partner grants HomeOfferFlow a non-exclusive,
   worldwide, royalty-free license during the term to host, reproduce, format
   for accessibility, display, distribute, and link approved Partner content to
   operate, promote, measure, and improve the placement. Partner retains its
   own content; HomeOfferFlow retains its platform, marketplace, brands, and
   aggregate analytics.

6. **Fees and cancellation.** Stripe payment records control payment
   processing. HomeOfferFlow may suspend or remove a placement for nonpayment,
   reversal, dispute, or expiration. Unless the Order Form states otherwise,
   either party may cancel a month-to-month placement on 30 days’ written
   notice. Cancellation ends future renewal after the paid period and does not
   create a pro-rata refund except as required by law or expressly stated.

7. **Data.** Each party independently controls data it collects. Partner gains
   no access to HomeOfferFlow user, offer, transaction, or account data under
   this agreement. Partner must comply with applicable consent, opt-out, email,
   text, telemarketing, privacy, and advertising requirements, and may not add
   users to marketing lists merely because they viewed or clicked a placement.

8. **Indemnity.** Partner will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless
   HomeOfferFlow and its personnel from third-party claims, losses, liabilities,
   costs, and reasonable attorney fees arising from Partner content, services,
   advertising, communications, data practices, licenses, professional conduct,
   breach, or allegations of infringement, deception, or illegality.

9. **Liability.** Except for payment and indemnity obligations, fraud, willful
   misconduct, or liability that law cannot limit, each party’s total liability
   is capped at the fees paid or payable under the relevant Order Form in the
   twelve months before the event. Neither party is liable for indirect,
   incidental, special, consequential, exemplary, punitive, lost-profit,
   lost-revenue, lost-data, or lost-business damages to the maximum extent law
   permits.

10. **General.** The parties are independent contractors. Texas law governs;
    exclusive venue is `[county], Texas`. Notices use the Order Form addresses.
    This agreement and the Order Form are the entire agreement and may be
    amended only in writing accepted by both parties. Electronic records and
    signatures are intended to be effective to the extent law permits.

**Partner acknowledgement.** By signing, Partner’s authorized representative
confirms authority to bind Partner and accepts this agreement. It becomes
effective when HomeOfferFlow accepts electronically, including by recording the
completed agreement or activating an approved placement. A completed agreement
is required before public placement.

- HomeOfferFlow’s legal entity, address, notice address, and Texas venue county.
- Exact renewal, cancellation, tax, refund, pilot, and exclusivity terms for
  the actual Stripe price configuration and each commercial tier.
- Category-specific license, advertising, referral-fee, anti-inducement, and
  insurance rules for regulated partners.
- Alignment with HomeOfferFlow’s Terms of Use, Privacy Notice, and any data or
  referral agreement.
