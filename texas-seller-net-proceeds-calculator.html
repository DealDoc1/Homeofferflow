Questions sellers ask

Clear limits make a better estimate.

Is this a final Texas seller net sheet?

No. It is a planning worksheet. A title company, lender payoff statement, contract, and final settlement statement control the actual figures.

Does HomeOfferFlow set default seller closing costs or commission?

No. You enter the figures you want to compare. Compensation, closing costs, and concessions are transaction-specific and negotiable.

Does this calculator collect my information?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. HomeOfferFlow does not ask for an address, email address, or login to use this worksheet.
