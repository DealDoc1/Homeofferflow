Founding partner questions

Clear commercial terms before you apply.

What is a HomeOfferFlow founding partner placement?

It is a labeled category-and-market placement for a Texas home-service business at relevant workflow moments. It is not a referral program, a required provider choice, or a guarantee of traffic, leads, transactions, or closings.

What happens after a founding partner application?

You submit the essentials, review the selected 90-day launch price and monthly renewal terms in secure Stripe checkout, then receive a private setup link after payment. A placement stays private until HomeOfferFlow reviews its details and the written placement agreement.

When does monthly founding partner billing begin?

The founding launch offer provides the first 90 days for the price of one standard month, with no setup fee. The selected plan then renews monthly unless canceled, as disclosed in secure checkout.
