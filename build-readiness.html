A step-by-step journey

From the first idea to move-in day.

Use these stages to organize the questions and conversations ahead. The right order and providers depend on your goals, finances, land, and timing.

STEP 1

Dream & discovery

Clarify location, budget range, timing, must-haves, and whether a custom build fits your plans.

STEP 2

Build readiness

Map the decisions ahead and gather the current-home, savings, and timeline details you want to discuss with qualified professionals.

STEP 3

Financing conversation

Ask a qualified lender to explain available financing paths, costs, timing, and requirements for your situation.

STEP 4

Land strategy

Compare location and land options, then confirm feasibility, utilities, restrictions, and costs with the appropriate professionals.

STEP 5

Builder & design

Compare builders, plans, written scopes, allowances, schedules, and responsibilities before making commitments.

STEP 6

Sell → build → transition

If you own a home now, coordinate its sale plan with financing, construction milestones, and any temporary housing needs.

STEP 7

Move-in day

Track the final dates and handoffs with your builder, lender, title provider, and other responsible parties.
