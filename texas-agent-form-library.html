Before you begin

Clear answers for agents and brokers.

Are released forms available to every signed-in agent?

Yes. The shared library is platform-wide for released workflows.

Does the library generate every Texas real estate form?

No. It identifies the live supported or review-only scope. Follow your brokerage-approved process for anything outside it.

What should I choose first?

Choose listing, buying, lease listing, or lease representation. Then answer only the questions for that transaction.

Can I start without payment?

Yes. Open a private workspace without payment. Eligible OnDemand Realty agents can review the separate 60-day plan.
