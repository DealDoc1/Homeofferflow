Common partner questions

Clear answers before you apply.

Is this a referral program?

No. It is labeled advertising inventory and never guarantees traffic, leads, transactions, or closings.

When does the 90-day launch begin?

When secure Stripe Checkout is completed. Public activation still follows private setup and written-agreement review.

Can I finish later?

Yes. Your essentials are saved on the device so you can return to the same checkout.
