Texas partner placement guide

Be findable when a home-service decision is already happening.

HomeOfferFlow gives Texas title companies, lenders, inspectors, contractors, and other providers visibility in the category directory. Only the single top active Premier partner across HomeOfferFlow is also shown in guided transaction workflows and after offer completion.

Review placementsCompare placement options

Advertising, clearly labeled. Partner placements are complimentary during beta and clearly labeled—not referrals, required selection, or promised leads. Users can choose any qualified provider or none.

How the placement works

1
Choose one category and one market.
Start with the service you provide and the Texas market where you want relevant visibility.
2
Compare the three tiers.
Founding and Featured profiles remain in the category directory. Premier appears first there; only the single top active Premier across HomeOfferFlow appears in guided transaction workflows and after offer completion. Each directory category shows no more than three partners.
3
Review secure terms.
All partner placements are complimentary during beta; no checkout, payment, or automatic paid conversion applies.
4
Complete private setup.
If approved, complete secure setup with your website, logo, market, and call to action. HomeOfferFlow reviews the details and written placement agreement before anything goes public.

Launch tiers at a glance

Founding Partner — complimentary during beta, with no automatic paid conversion.
Category directory profile, neutral details and contact options, and view/click reporting. Founding profiles do not appear in guided workflows.
Featured Partner — complimentary during beta, with no automatic paid conversion.
Everything in Founding plus a more visible directory position and enhanced profile or scheduling CTA. Featured Partners do not appear in guided workflows.
Premier Partner — complimentary during beta, with no automatic paid conversion.
Premier appears first in its category directory. Only the single top active Premier across HomeOfferFlow appears in guided workflows and after offer completion; up to two other profiles may appear in that category directory.

Keep the first step simple

The no-charge application needs only your company, business contact, category, and primary market. If you leave before submitting, the essentials can be resumed on the same device without creating a duplicate application.

Common partner questions

Clear answers before you apply.

Is this a referral program?

No. It is labeled advertising inventory and never guarantees traffic, leads, transactions, or closings.

Are partner placements free during beta?

No fee is charged during beta and no automatic paid conversion applies. Public activation still follows private setup and written-agreement review.

Will there be an automatic paid conversion?

No. There is no fee during beta and no automatic paid conversion. Any future pricing requires a separate owner decision and clear terms before enrollment.
