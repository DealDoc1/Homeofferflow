Before you open the workspace

Clear answers for agents and brokers.

Do I need a password?

No. HomeOfferFlow uses a secure sign-in link for the agent or broker workspace.

Can I use saved defaults for the next offer?

Yes. The workspace supports reusable agent and brokerage defaults, while a new offer clears prior client and property details.

Can I organize a seller-side listing?

Enrolled brokerage agents can use private seller leads and listing workspaces to organize launch planning, a seller consultation brief, and offer comparisons. Seller form generation and signing remain separately source-gated.

Can I prepare a buyer relationship draft?

Yes. The workspace has private drafts for buyer representation, unrepresented showing, and consumer-notice workflows. They remain preview-only until their separate signing QA is complete.

Does this replace broker supervision?

No. HomeOfferFlow is workflow software. You remain responsible for reviewing terms, following brokerage policy, and using the appropriate approved process for forms outside the live scope.
