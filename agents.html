Before you open the workspace

Clear answers for agents and brokers.

Do I need a password?

No. HomeOfferFlow uses a secure sign-in link for the agent or broker workspace.

What happens after I choose a transaction?

Purchase lets you choose a purchase offer, buyer representation agreement, addendum, or customer showing form. Property listing lets you choose a listing task, seller disclosure, or buyer-offer comparison. Lease listing goes directly to the landlord and property details. Tenant representation lets you choose a tenant representation agreement or customer showing form. The interview then asks only for the details that apply.

Can I use saved defaults for the next offer?

Yes. The workspace supports reusable agent details and, when applicable, brokerage defaults, while a new offer clears prior client and property details.

Are shared forms available to every signed-in agent?

Yes. Every available form in HomeOfferFlow's shared agent library is open to every signed-in agent; forms are not assigned one brokerage or agent at a time.

Can I organize a seller-side listing?

Yes. You do not need a brokerage seat to create your own seller or lease-listing workspace. Use it to organize launch planning, a seller consultation brief, and offer comparisons.

Can I prepare documents from the shared library?

Yes. Every signed-in agent can prepare available relationship, consumer-notice, mineral-reservation, seller-financing, environmental, appraisal, and lease documents from the shared library. Each document clearly states whether it is ready for review or signature sending.

Does this replace broker supervision?

No. HomeOfferFlow is workflow software. You remain responsible for reviewing terms and following applicable professional requirements.
