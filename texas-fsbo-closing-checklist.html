Under contract? Keep the closing path clear and organized.

Keep this lightweight: start with the signed contract, the deadline list, and the contact details for the people already involved. This guide helps you organize—not replace title, legal, lending, brokerage, or other professional work.

A five-part closing checklist

1
Put the core contract facts in one place.
Keep the executed contract, important dates, closing location or contact, and your current list of questions together.
2
Make follow-ups visible.
Track the conversations or documents related to inspections, repairs, financing, title, disclosures, survey, association items, and possession. Mark unknowns rather than guessing.
3
Use the right person for each answer.
Route title, lending, legal, brokerage, and other regulated questions to the appropriate responsible professional.
4
Confirm closing logistics directly.
Verify the time, place, identification and document expectations, and any payment or wire instructions directly with the title company or other responsible party using a trusted contact method.
5
Review the finish line before closing.
Keep a final list of open items, possession details, and questions to raise with the appropriate professional before you sign.
