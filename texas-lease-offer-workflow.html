Common lease workflow questions

Clear answers before you begin.

Should I choose lease listing or lease representation first?

Yes. Start with the transaction type so the workspace can keep property-owner and tenant-representation questions separate.

What should I have ready?

Keep the property address, parties, proposed dates, rent, deposits, utilities, pets, and confirmed instructions available. Mark unknown items for follow-up instead of guessing.

Does HomeOfferFlow select a lease form for me?

No. It identifies the workflow and supported or review-only scope. Agents remain responsible for the brokerage-approved process and supervision.

Can I start without payment?

Yes. Agents and brokers can open a private workspace without payment, then sign in by secure link to continue.
