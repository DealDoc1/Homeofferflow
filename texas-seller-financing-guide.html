Texas seller financing workflow

Organize the seller-financing conversation before the paperwork.

Seller financing can raise financing, lien, timing, and documentation questions quickly. Start by organizing what the parties have confirmed, mark the open questions, and make the private review step clear.

A simple four-step order

1
Confirm the transaction.
Identify the property and parties, and confirm that seller financing is actually being considered.
2
Keep confirmed facts together.
Organize financing terms, payment timing, lien information, and confirmed instructions. Do not fill gaps by guessing.
3
Mark open questions.
Separate questions for the client, broker, lender, attorney, title company, or another qualified professional.
4
Prepare the right review step.
Signed-in agents can use the released TXR-1914 private-review workflow, then follow the brokerage-approved process for review and any separate delivery or signature action.

Before private review

Clear answers for agents and brokers.

Does this guide provide seller-financing advice?

No. It is a workflow guide only. Use your brokerage-approved process and the appropriate qualified professionals for terms, tax, lending, title, and legal questions.

Can an agent prepare the TXR-1914 review draft?

Yes. Every signed-in agent can start the released private-review workflow from the shared library. The draft stays private and does not create loan documents or a signature request.

What should be confirmed before private review?

Keep the confirmed property, parties, financing terms, payment timing, lien information, and unresolved questions organized. Mark unknowns for follow-up instead of guessing.
