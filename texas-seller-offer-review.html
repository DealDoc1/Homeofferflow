What to compare in a Texas offer.

1
Price and estimated net.
Keep price, concessions, expected costs, and the questions that belong with the appropriate title or brokerage professional together.
2
Financing and earnest money.
Note financing type, approval context, earnest money, option fee, and deadlines that need confirmation from the buyer, lender, or responsible professional.
3
Timing and possession.
Compare closing date, possession, leaseback or occupancy questions, and any timing that affects your next move.
4
Contingencies and concessions.
List inspection, sale-of-property, appraisal, repair, and concession terms so the complete package—not just price—is visible for review.

A simple seller-review checklist.

  • Save each offer with the property and buyer identifiers you need for your own records.
  • Compare price, financing, timing, concessions, contingencies, possession, and estimated net together.
  • Write down unclear terms and route them to the appropriate licensed, title, legal, lending, or other professional.
  • Do not treat a worksheet or summary as acceptance, rejection, negotiation, or a contract decision.
